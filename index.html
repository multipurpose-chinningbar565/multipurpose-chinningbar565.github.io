🚀 homebrew-lidrun - Keep Mac active with lid closed

Download LidRun Now

LidRun helps your Mac stay awake and run background tasks even when you close the screen. Many users need to keep AI agents, code compilers, or data processes active while they travel or move away from their workspace. Usually, closing the lid puts a Mac into sleep mode. LidRun prevents this sleep mode while protecting your hardware.

🛡️ Why Use LidRun?

Computers produce heat when they work hard. If you close your laptop, the device might overheat because it cannot vent air properly. LidRun monitors your battery temperature and power levels. If your device reaches a dangerous heat level, the app stops the background work to prevent hardware damage. This tool keeps your machine safe while it finishes your jobs.

⚙️ Setting Guardrails

LidRun uses internal sensors to check your Mac. By default, the app tracks the thermal state. If the temperature crosses 80 degrees Celsius, the app pauses your active processes. It resumes them once the system cools down. You can adjust these settings by clicking the menu bar icon and selecting Preferences.

  • Thermal Guard: Automatically pauses jobs if the CPU gets too hot.
  • Battery Guard: Stops background tasks if your battery falls below 20%.
  • Menu Bar Status: Shows you at a glance if your Mac is allowed to sleep or stay awake.
